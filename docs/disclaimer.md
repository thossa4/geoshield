## Informational-use statement

GeoShield Home Passport is an informational resilience-screening and
decision-support product. It is not, and should not be relied upon as:

- An official flood determination or flood zone certification.
- An elevation certificate, land survey, or engineering inspection.
- A home inspection, structural assessment, or appraisal.
- An environmental assessment.
- An insurance quote, underwriting decision, or premium prediction.
- A guarantee that a property will or will not flood, or that any
  mitigation action will prevent loss.
- A determination of eligibility for any grant, program, or incentive.

Findings are based on publicly available and user-submitted data as of
the report date shown. Government datasets and flood maps change; a
report reflects conditions and sources known at the time it was
generated and may not reflect current conditions.

## Professional verification

Where GeoShield identifies a concern or recommends an action, it is
directing the user toward verification by an appropriately licensed or
qualified professional (surveyor, engineer, home inspector, roofing
contractor, drainage contractor, etc.), not substituting for that
professional's judgment. See `Phase 12, Step 12.4` in the source
blueprint for the specific split between what GeoShield does and what a
professional should do.

## Marketplace referral

GeoShield may connect users with third-party service providers.
Providers listed as "verified" have supplied documentation described in
the provider verification policy; this is not a guarantee of the
quality, licensing status, or outcome of any work performed. Providers
cannot pay to influence a property's GeoShield rating.

## User-submitted data

Any property attribute entered by the user (e.g., roof age, FORTIFIED
status) is labeled as unverified unless independently confirmed, and is
used only to personalize guidance — never to silently override
authoritative-source indicators.
